## Established — must be cited, not claimed

1. **Kagome isostaticity and marginal mechanics.** Periodic kagome is the
   canonical locally isostatic 2D lattice (z = 2d = 4, Maxwell); its floppy
   modes and topological boundary mechanics are a developed field:
   Kane & Lubensky, Nat. Phys. 10, 39 (2014) [arXiv:1308.0554] and
   descendants. Our finding that the K=2 packing is rigid only at contact is
   the packing-realisation of known marginality — confirmation, not novelty.
2. **Mechanically stable vacancy-depleted crystals in 3D.** Torquato &
   Stillinger, J. Appl. Phys. 102, 093511 (2007) [arXiv:0707.4263]:
   "tunneled crystals" — strictly jammed FCC/HCP sub-packings with chains of
   vacancies, phi down to 2pi/9 ~ 0.4937. Also mechanically stable packings
   at vanishing density ("Dionysian", Dennis & Corwin, PRL 125, 118002
   (2020)). Any 3D claim must be: the TANGENTIAL selection (eta = Q_iso,
   every Voronoi neighbour a contact) picks a specific modular-rule family
   out of this known class — not that rigid depleted packings exist.
   (Handoff README already flags Donev–Torquato–Stillinger
   cond-mat/0408550 as prior art for the K=4 structure.)
3. **The maple-leaf lattice** is standard in frustrated magnetism as the
   1/7-depleted triangular lattice (unit cell of six, z = 5; e.g.
   PRB 104, 224415 (2021); PRB 111, 094439 (2025); name due to Betts).
   Known as a spin lattice, NOT as a disk packing.
